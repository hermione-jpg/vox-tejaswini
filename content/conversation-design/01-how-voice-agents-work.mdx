### 1. Understanding the Users

**User:** Hindi-speaking farmers who use voice-based services to access agricultural information. They may speak standard Hindi, regional dialects or a natural mix of Hindi and English. Their familiarity with technology, digital services and agricultural terminology may vary.

**User Needs**
- Access reliable agricultural information and government schemes in a familiar language.
- Ask questions naturally, without technological complications.
- Get practical information about seeds, crops, cultivation and prices.
- Understand quantities, measurements and costs.
- Receive explanations that are easy to follow and act upon.

**User Pain Points**
- Difficulty expressing queries in formal or technical language.
- Misinterpretation of numbers, units and quantities.
- Unclear or overly technical answers.
- Difficulty correcting information or recovering from conversational misunderstandings.

### 2. Defining the Assistant's Personality

**Persona: A Knowledgeable and Approachable Agricultural Guide**

The assistant should communicate like someone who understands everyday agricultural concerns and can explain information clearly.

**Language and Tone**
- Primarily conversational Hindi, accommodating natural English terms.
- Short, direct sentences with familiar vocabulary.
- Respectful forms of address.
- Natural acknowledgements and transitions.
- Explain technical agricultural terms when first mentioned and when required.

### 3. Selecting the Use Case

**Use Case: Estimating Wheat Seed Requirements and Cost**

The conversation focuses on helping farmers estimate the quantity of wheat seed required for their land and the approximate cost of purchasing it.

**User Need:** Farmers need a simple way to understand how much seed they should purchase for their land and how much it is likely to cost, without having to perform the calculations themselves.

**Assistant Goal** Collect the necessary information, retrieve relevant agricultural and pricing data, interpret the farmer's measurements accurately and communicate a clear estimate.

**Scope of the Conversation**
- Identify the crop and farmer's location.
- Retrieve the relevant seed rate, package weight and price.
- Understand the farmer's land area and measurement unit.
- Calculate the required seed quantity.
- Determine the number of bags required and estimated cost.
- Handle unclear measurements, corrections and missing information.
