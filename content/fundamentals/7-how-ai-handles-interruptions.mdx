Conversations don't always happen one person at a time.

Sometimes we remember something halfway through.

Sometimes we change our minds.

Sometimes we interrupt before the other person has finished speaking.

These interruptions are a natural part of everyday conversations.

Voice AI needs to handle them just as naturally.

## Interruptions in Voice AI

In Voice AI, the ability to interrupt the assistant while it's speaking is called barge-in.
When a user starts speaking before the AI has finished its response, the assistant pauses, listens, and shifts its attention to the user.

This allows the conversation to continue without waiting for the AI to finish talking.

## Why is barge-in important?

Imagine asking:

"Book a table for two tomorrow."

The AI begins responding.

Before it finishes, you say:

"Actually, make it four."

A good voice assistant immediately stops speaking and listens to your correction.

Without barge-in, you'd have to wait for the AI to finish before speaking again, making the conversation feel slow and unnatural.

**How does AI know you've interrupted?**

Even while speaking, the AI continues listening through the microphone.

If it detects that you've started talking, it decides whether to:

Stop speaking and listen.

Ignore the sound if it's background noise.

Finish its sentence before responding.

Making the right decision helps conversations feel smooth and gives users more control.
