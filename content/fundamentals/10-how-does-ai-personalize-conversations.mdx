Two people can ask an AI the exact same question and get different answers — not because the AI is guessing, but because it's using context beyond the words themselves.

## What personalization actually uses

Personalization isn't one feature. It's a combination of signals working together:

- Past conversations and preferences
- Account or profile information
- Current context (time, location, device)
- Behavior within the current session

## Why this matters more in voice

In text interfaces, users can scroll back and re-read. In voice, there's no scrollback — the AI has to carry the right context forward on its own, or the conversation feels like talking to a stranger every time.

<Callout>
Personalization isn't about sounding friendly. It's about not making the user repeat themselves.
</Callout>

## The tradeoff: personalization vs. privacy

Every piece of remembered context is also a piece of stored data. Good voice AI design makes deliberate choices about:

- What's worth remembering
- How long to keep it
- How to let users see or clear it

This is why "smart" and "trustworthy" have to be designed together, not treated as separate problems.
