At first, that sounds simple. But underneath that simple interaction, your brain is performing one of the most complex tasks humans can do.

Every conversation follows four steps:

- Someone has an intention
- They express it
- Someone understands it
- Someone responds

Everything in conversational AI is built on these four steps.

## Humans communicate in many ways

Speaking is only one form of communication. We also communicate through:

- Speech
- Listening
- Facial expressions
- Gestures
- Writing
- Reading
- Touch
- Emotions
- Silence

In real conversations, many of these happen at the same time.

## How humans communicate with computers

For decades, digital interfaces were limited to reading, writing, touch, and visual feedback.

We clicked buttons, filled forms, typed on keyboards, and navigated screens.

Now, computers are starting to understand more natural signals:

- Speaking
- Listening
- Pauses and silence
- Interruptions
- Emotion (to a limited extent)

This shift is what makes conversational and voice AI possible.

## What happens when someone speaks?

To understand conversational AI, we first need to understand what your brain is doing during a conversation.

<Callout>
Stephen C. Levinson & Francisco Torreira's research on conversational turn-taking has found that the average gap between one person finishing a sentence and another beginning to speak is around 200 milliseconds (0.2 seconds).

This is so fast that your brain begins planning a response before the other person has finished speaking. 
</Callout>

Even though it feels effortless, your brain is doing multiple things at once:

- Detect sound
- Recognize speech
- Identify words
- Understand meaning
- Connect context
- Decide what to do
- Plan a response
- Speak

All of this happens in a fraction of a second.

> Words aren't the whole conversation

Your brain also reads subtle signals beyond words:

- Tone
- Pitch
- Volume
- Speaking speed
- Pauses
- Emphasis
- Facial expressions
- Emotion

These signals help us understand confidence, uncertainty, excitement, sarcasm, frustration, and many other emotions that words alone cannot convey.

This is one of the reasons conversations between humans feel natural, while conversations with AI can still feel mechanical.
